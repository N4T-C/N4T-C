## 🌟 Featured Projects

### 🍔 EZFOODZ
Intra-campus food delivery platform connecting students with campus restaurants via mobile app + restaurant dashboard. Supports student delivery partners for part-time income.

`React` `FastAPI` `Firebase` `Groq API` `Flutter` `SQLite` `Axios`

🔗 Repo: https://github.com/natcssn/ezfoodz-official

---

### 📄 Spellfolio — AI-Powered ATS Resume Builder
Analyzes resumes and generates ATS-optimized content. Automates structuring, keyword optimization, and formatting — reduces resume prep time by ~60%.

`Python` `Django` `Django REST Framework` `SQLite` `Chart.js` `HTML/CSS/JS`

---

### 🔥 FlowMotion — Intelligent Habit & Productivity Tracker
Linux-based habit tracking with visual analytics dashboards. Features a Duolingo-inspired emotional notification engine for streak-based habit adherence.

`Next.js` `TypeScript` `Google Gemini AI` `Firebase` `Cloud Firestore` `Tailwind CSS`

---

### 🎓 EdTek — AI-Adaptive Learning & Assessment Platform
AI-driven platform that adapts content difficulty based on student performance. Auto-generates quizzes, question papers for teachers, and cheat-sheets from uploaded study materials.

`Next.js` `TypeScript` `Google Gemini AI` `Firebase Auth` `Firestore` `Tailwind CSS` `pdfjs-dist`

---

### 🎬 YouTube Shorts Video Automator — Discord Bot
Automated pipeline converting user-submitted links + captions into fully edited ranking-style YouTube Shorts. Cuts editing time from ~30 minutes to under 2 minutes.

`Python` `Discord.py` `FFmpeg` `Instaloader` `Automation Scripts`

---

### 🚦 Smart Traffic Management System
Scheduling-algorithm-based traffic simulation for 4-way intersections. Models signal strategies to reduce vehicle wait times and improve throughput. Smart India Hackathon project.

`Python` `Simulation Algorithms` `Scheduling Algorithms` `Replit`

🔗 Repo: https://github.com/N4T-C/smart-traffic-management-simulation
